FALSE POSITIVES 

Give the machine a photograph or a short video. It segments the vegetation and the people it finds, then fills the rest of the frame with detections of air pollution it cannot see. The pollution figures are real. The detections are not.

$ ./false_positives --source=local --air=live --truth=none

[ drop image or video ]

Or click to choose a file. Video is cut to the first 15 seconds. Nothing is uploaded: the model runs in your browser.

Segmentation: SegFormer trained on ADE20K, running locally through Transformers.js. Air data: Copernicus atmosphere model via the Open-Meteo air quality API, labelled as modelled because no station measured this frame.
